- [x] **Universal Office Export:** Browser-native generation of `.xlsx` spreadsheets, `.pptx` slide decks, Word `.docx`, and print-ready PDFs.
- [x] **Autonomous Morning Cron Dispatch:** Scheduled 8:00 AM strategic intelligence audits delivered directly to Telegram.

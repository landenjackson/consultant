Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026  |  Platform: Consultant Studio (www.consultant-studio.app)

1. Client-Side Document & Data Architecture

Consultant Studio operates on a client-first privacy model. When you upload Word (.docx), PDF, or spreadsheet (.xlsx) files, documents are processed entirely in-memory inside your local browser runtime. Raw documents, saved strategy memos, and private P&L telemetry are stored locally in your browser's encrypted local storage (`localStorage`) and are never written to a centralized database.

2. Processing of Inquiries & AI API Transmission

When you submit a prompt or document for analysis, the extracted text is securely transmitted via TLS 1.3 encryption to enterprise Google Cloud / AI model inference endpoints solely to generate the requested operational analysis. Prompts and documents are not retained or utilized to train general AI models.

3. Billing & Payment Information

All subscription billing and financial transactions are handled securely by Stripe, Inc. (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant). Consultant Studio does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers or banking credentials.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. Clearing your browser's local storage or clicking "Clear Vault" inside the application immediately and irreversibly deletes all stored records, strategy memos, and session profiles.

For privacy inquiries, technical support, or enterprise governance questions, please contact our support desk at support@consultant-studio.app or reach out directly to Landen Jackson at jacksonlanden3986@gmail.com.
